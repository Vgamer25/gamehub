PRIVACY POLICY

1. Information We Collect

IP Addresses: We collect and process your IP address automatically when you access Gamehub. This data is used for security monitoring, fraud prevention, service functionality, and ensuring compliance with our Terms of Service.

Email Addresses: We collect and store the email address you provide during registration or authentication. Gamehub administrators can view and process your email address to manage accounts, provide support, and enforce platform rules.

2. How We Use Your Information

To deliver and maintain platform features.

To detect, prevent, and mitigate security threats, phishing attempts, or unauthorized access.

To enforce our Terms of Service, including identifying forbidden proxy or VPN usage.

3. Data Protection and Access
Your data is accessed only by authorized Gamehub administrators and is maintained to safeguard against unauthorized access or disclosure. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.
